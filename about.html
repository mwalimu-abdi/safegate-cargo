About Us

Pamoja Cargo is a trusted international logistics and freight forwarding company dedicated to connecting global markets with East Africa. We specialize in the safe, efficient, and reliable transportation of cargo from major global hubs including Turkey, Dubai, China, India, the United States, Thailand, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and Canada.

Our operations are carefully designed to ensure smooth delivery into Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, and Somalia, serving both individuals and businesses of all sizes. Whether you are shipping personal goods, commercial merchandise, or specialized cargo, Pamoja Cargo provides tailored logistics solutions that prioritize safety, speed, and transparency.

Driven by professionalism and customer trust, our mission is to simplify international shipping by removing complexity, reducing delays, and offering dependable service at every stage of the journey — from collection to final delivery.
