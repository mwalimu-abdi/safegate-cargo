Our Services

At Pamoja Cargo, we provide comprehensive logistics and cargo transportation solutions designed to meet the growing demands of individuals, businesses, and organizations across East Africa. Our services are built on reliability, safety, and efficiency, ensuring that every shipment — large or small — reaches its destination in perfect condition and on schedule.

We handle a wide range of cargo types, including personal belongings, commercial merchandise, industrial materials, and specialized shipments. With a strong international network and experienced logistics professionals, we manage the entire shipping process from origin to final delivery, allowing our clients to focus on their core activities with confidence.

Cargo Destinations & Origin Countries
Pamoja Cargo connects global markets to East Africa through well-established shipping routes from major origin countries such as Dubai, China, India, Turkey, the United States, Thailand, Canada, and Saudi Arabia. We ensure smooth and secure delivery to key destinations in Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, and Somalia — including Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Eldoret, Dar es Salaam, Kampala, and Mogadishu. No matter where your goods originate, we guarantee seamless handling, customs coordination, and timely delivery.

Fragile Cargo

We specialize in the careful handling and transportation of fragile and sensitive items such as glassware, electronics, artwork, and precision equipment. Our team uses protective packaging, secure loading methods, and controlled handling processes to minimize risk and ensure your valuable items arrive safely and intact.
